How and when is my order delivered?
Delivery is available 24/7 on WhatsApp. Most packages arrive as an activation, an account, or a product key. Each product page states its exact delivery type and what you need to provide, under "Needed from you".

Why do USD and USDT prices change slightly?
PKR is the base price. USD and USDT follow a live rate, so a converted price can move a little when the rate moves. The PKR price does not change with the rate. Use the currency switcher at the top of the page to switch between PKR, USD and USDT.

How do the cart and offers work?
Add packages to the cart, then send one WhatsApp message for the whole cart. Offers are counted per product: a reward applies when one product reaches its threshold, and one offer applies per product. The cart page shows the quantity, the free items, and the total before you send.

Do I need to give my email?
Some products need your email or account details, others need nothing at all. Every product page states this in advance in its "Needed from you" line.
