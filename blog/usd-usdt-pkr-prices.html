PKR, USD and USDT Prices: How the Switcher Works

Published 2026-10-25 · 5 min read

Prices start in PKR

Every package price on the store is listed in PKR first. The card on the home page, the package page and the cart all show the PKR price by default. PKR is the base currency of the store, and every other display is converted from it. When you open the store for the first time, you see PKR prices, and the toolbar shows the currency switcher with PKR selected.

The switcher sits in the toolbar near the top of every page. It offers three choices: PKR, USD and USDT. Tap a choice and every price on the page changes to that currency at once: the cards, the package pages, the cart rows and the cart total. You do not need to reload the page, and you do not need to change the currency on each page separately. One tap covers the whole store.

Your choice is remembered on your device

Once you pick a currency, the store remembers it on your device. Close the page and come back later, and the same currency is still selected. The memory lives in your browser on that device, so a different device or a different browser starts again from PKR. Clearing your browser storage also resets the choice to PKR.

This memory is a display setting. It changes the display currency. It does not change the package, the duration or the delivery type, and it does not place an order.

USD and USDT follow a live exchange rate

When you switch to USD or USDT, the store converts the PKR price using a live exchange rate. The rate comes from a feed the store loads after the page opens. A line in the toolbar shows the current rates once the feed arrives. Because the feed is live, the converted figures can move slightly from day to day, even when the PKR price of a package stays the same. A package priced in PKR does not change when the rate moves; the USD or USDT figure shown beside it does, because the conversion uses the fresh rate. If you compare a USD figure from yesterday with one from today, a small difference comes from the rate, not from a price change on the package. The toolbar rate line lets you see the rate behind the figures on any visit.

Rounding keeps the figures tidy

Converted prices are rounded upward to the cent in USD and USDT. A conversion that lands on a long fraction is shortened to cents, so the figures stay readable. The rounding is applied the same way on cards, package pages and the cart, so the figure you see on a card matches the figure on its package page and the line total in the cart.

Cart totals follow the same rule. Each line total is converted and rounded, and the cart total is the sum of the displayed line totals in the selected currency. The total you see in the cart is the total that goes into the WhatsApp message. There is no separate total at checkout, because the store has no checkout of its own: the WhatsApp message is the order.

The WhatsApp message uses your selected currency

When you tap an order button, the WhatsApp message carries the price in the currency you selected. If PKR is selected, the message shows the PKR amount. If USD is selected, it shows the USD amount, and the same holds for USDT. The message names the tool, the package and the duration beside the amount, so the seller sees the full order in one place.

Confirm the final amount in the chat. The seller confirms availability and the next steps there, and the chat is where any question about the amount is settled. If the rate moved between your visit and your message, the seller confirms the amount that applies. Keep the chat as the place where the final figure is agreed, and do not rely on a figure you saw on an earlier visit.

A few practical points

First, check the toolbar before you order. The selected currency sits there, and a glance confirms it. Second, when you compare two packages, compare them in the same currency. Switching mid comparison mixes two conversions and makes the comparison harder to read. Third, when you use the cart, switch currency before you tap the order button if you want the message in a different currency. The cart re-renders everything in the new currency at once, including the total.

Fourth, remember that the rate line appears after the feed loads. On a slow connection the page may show PKR figures for a moment before the conversion applies. Wait for the rate line if you want the converted figures, and check that the currency label in the toolbar matches your choice. Fifth, if you share a package page with someone, tell them which currency you were viewing, because their device starts from PKR and may show a different figure for the same package.

You can try the switcher on any page. Browse the AI chatbots and assistants or design and creativity sections, tap the switcher in the toolbar, and watch the card prices change.
