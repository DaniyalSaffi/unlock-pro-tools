Delivery Type and Requirements on a Package Page

Published 2026-10-25 · 5 min read

What the two lines mean

Every package page carries two lines that describe the handover. The delivery type line states how the package reaches you. The requirements line states what the seller needs from you before delivery. Read both lines before you order, so the order does not stall for a missing detail.

Most delivery types end with the words via WhatsApp. That means the delivery steps happen in the WhatsApp chat where you placed the order. A link, a key, account details or an activation step arrives there, not by email and not on the site itself. Keep the chat open until the seller confirms the delivery. One delivery type breaks that pattern, and it is covered below.

Activation deliveries

The most common delivery type is activation through WhatsApp. The seller activates the plan and walks you through the steps in the chat. Close variants of the same idea appear on other pages. An activation link means the chat carries a link that completes the activation. A premium link and an offer link work the same way: the link arrives in the chat and you open it to reach the premium state. An activation code means the chat carries a code rather than a link. Activation on your own number means the activation is tied to a phone number you provide.

A combined form, account and activation through WhatsApp, means the delivery mixes account details with an activation step. The seller sends the details and the activation follows in the same chat.

Account and key deliveries

Another large group sends you something to sign in with. Account details through WhatsApp means the seller sends the account information in the chat. Account login details and plain login details mean the same in practice: the chat carries what you need to sign in. A ready private account means you receive an account that is already set up and private to you.

Product key deliveries send a key instead of an account. A product key through WhatsApp means the chat carries a key you enter to activate the package. Key and activation, activation and key, and a VPN key are variants of the same idea. A coupon code delivery sends a code you redeem. A redeem link sends a link that performs the redemption. Credits details and credits and activation cover packages measured in credits: the chat carries the credit details, sometimes with an activation step beside them.

Admin, API and QR deliveries

A smaller group covers admin and technical handovers. Admin access setup, admin panel access and admin account details mean the seller sets up admin access and sends the details in the chat. API details and API key and credit details mean the chat carries API information, sometimes with credit details beside it.

QR deliveries use a QR step. A QR process means the login or activation runs through a QR scan, with the steps given in the chat. QR login details means the chat carries the QR login information. An OTP code delivery sends a one time code in the chat. A bundle details delivery sends the details of a bundle package in the chat.

The one exception

One delivery type does not run through WhatsApp. Recharge on your own account means the recharge is applied to an account you already hold. You provide the account, and the seller applies the recharge to it.

One package shows no delivery type at all. The line reads "Not stated" on that page. If you want that package, ask the seller in the chat how it will be delivered before you order. A "Not stated" line is not a promise about the method, and a short question settles it.

What you need to provide

The requirements line is usually short. The most common requirements line asks for your email address. Send the address the package should use, and double check its spelling. A mistyped address is the most common cause of a stalled delivery. Some pages ask for your Gmail address, or for your Gmail or Google account, which means the same address you use for Google services. Other pages ask for a service specific address: your Spotify email, your Claude account email, your ElevenLabs email, your Notion email or your LinkedIn email.

A few pages ask for something else: your phone number, or your Discord name and server. Send exactly what the line asks for, nothing extra. The remaining pages ask for nothing at all. Those pages state that you receive a ready account, a product key or the details, and you provide nothing before delivery. When the line says you provide nothing, do not send personal details anyway.

Before you order

Match the two lines with your situation. If the requirements line asks for an email address, have that address ready before you tap the order button. If the delivery type is a key or a code, expect to enter it yourself after the chat delivers it. If the delivery type reads "Not stated", ask first.
